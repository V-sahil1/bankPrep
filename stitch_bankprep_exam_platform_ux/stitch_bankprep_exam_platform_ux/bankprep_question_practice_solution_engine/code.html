A train 240 m long crosses another train 300 m long traveling in the opposite direction on parallel tracks in 18 seconds. If the speed of the first train is 60 km/h, what is the speed of the second train (in km/h)?

train Train 1 (L1 = 240m, S1 = 60 km/h) → ← Train 2 (L2 = 300m, S2 = ? km/h) train
Opposite Directions
t = 18s
Total Crossing Distance = L1 + L2 Relative Speed = S1 + S2
verified
Correct Answer: Option B (48 km/h) You took 48 seconds (Average candidate time: 62 seconds)
+14s Faster than avg

analytics Comprehensive Mathematical Derivation

Standard Approach
STEP 01 Combined Length

Total Relative Distance = $L_1 + L_2$

240 m + 300 m = 540 meters
STEP 02 Relative Speed (m/s)

$\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Time}}$

540 / 18 = 30 m/s
STEP 03 Conversion to km/h

Multiply m/s by factor $(\frac{18}{5})$

30 × (18 / 5) = 6 × 18 = 108 km/h
STEP 04 Solve for Unknown Speed

Opposite Direction: $S_1 + S_2 = S_{\text{rel}}$

60 + S₂ = 108 → S₂ = 48 km/h
bolt Pro Shortcut / 15-Second Exam Trick

Direct ratio method in banking prelims:
$\frac{L_1 + L_2}{t} \times \frac{18}{5} = S_1 + S_2 \implies \frac{540}{18} \times \frac{18}{5} \implies \frac{540}{5} = 108\text{ km/h}$.
Direct mental subtraction: 108 − 60 = 48 km/h without writing intermediate steps.

warning
Common Candidate Trap to Avoid:

When two bodies travel in opposite directions, their speeds always add up ($S_1 + S_2$). Candidates frequently apply the same-direction formula ($S_1 - S_2$), arriving incorrectly at Option D (56 km/h) or Option C.
