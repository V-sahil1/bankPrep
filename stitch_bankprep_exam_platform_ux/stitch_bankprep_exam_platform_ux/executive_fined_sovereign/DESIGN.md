## Brand & Style

This design system targets C-suite leaders, senior wealth managers, and institutional financial learners who demand precision, clarity, and authority without corporate dryness. The visual voice merges high-stakes editorial journalism with modern quantitative computing: direct, commanding, yet tailored with bespoke artisanal accents.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Corporate Architectural Precision with Editorial Signature**:
- **Structured Precision**: Tall, condensed typographic scales evoke financial tickers, institutional ledgers, and terminal architecture.
- **Editorial Distinction**: Fluid script accents act like an executive endorsement or a professor's signature, breaking computational rigidity with human intent.
- **Confidence & Economy**: Interfaces minimize decorative clutter, favoring rigorous white space, hairline rules, and high-impact structural contrast.

## Colors

The palette establishes institutional trust anchored by an electric executive blue and deep slate foundations:

- **Primary (`#2563eb`)**: Sovereign Blue. Used selectively for key interactive triggers, selected states, dynamic progress trackers, and critical quantitative focal points.
- **Secondary (`#0f172a`)**: Midnight Slate. Provides deep, immutable structural weight across primary headings, dominant containers, and top-tier navigation.
- **Tertiary (`#0d9488`)**: Fiscal Jade. Highlights financial gain metrics, certification completions, and verified institutional benchmarks.
- **Neutral (`#64748b`)**: Steel Gray. Formulates sub-metrics, table borders, and structural framing.

Surface layers operate on subtle warm whites (`#f8fafc` to `#ffffff`) with ultra-fine border lines (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`), preventing stark hospital-white glare while maintaining high executive contrast.

## Typography

The typographical triad balances structural impact, crystalline comprehension, and human executive nuance:

- **Oswald (Headlines, Quantitative Metrics, Labels)**: Condensed, architectural, and definitive. Render all uppercase section labels, metrics, indices, and hero headings in Oswald to establish an institutional terminal presence.
- **Playwrite IT Moderna (Signature Accents)**: Strictly reserved for short, emotive phrases—such as executive module greetings, dean's notes, honorary certificate seals, or strategic insight prompts. Never use for more than 4–6 consecutive words or for functional UI states.
- **Work Sans (Body Text & Documentation)**: Crisp, modern, low-distortion grotesk delivering neutral, effortless legibility across complex curriculum notes, financial statements, and regulatory breakdowns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a rigorous 12-column responsive grid engineered for multi-column dashboards, case study sidebars, and analytical telemetry:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum content container width capped at 1320px for optimal long-form legibility.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Complex metrics cards reflow into 2x2 modular matrix formations.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Data columns stack vertically, while numerical metric cards transition into horizontal scroll carousels or concise list items.

Layout spacing strictly adopts multiples of 4px/8px, using consistent inner card padding (`space-lg`) to reflect institutional calm.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids theatrical 3D drops or cloudy skeuomorphism, relying instead on structural planar elevation:

- **Planar Hierarchy**: Depth is communicated predominantly through crisp low-contrast hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) coupled with tinted background tone steps (White `#FFFFFF` over Base Canvas `#F8FAFC`).
- **Tactile Hover & Popovers**: Interactive cards, active cohorts, and modal layers employ a subtle, high-density shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Active Focus & Selections**: Active elevation is defined by an ink-stroke border highlight in Sovereign Blue (`#2563eb`), rather than excessive elevation drift.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a precise, calibrated 8px baseline radius (`ROUND_EIGHT`), imparting a structured yet engineered feel:

- **Default UI (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Buttons, inputs, metric tiles, badges, and curriculum cohort cards.
- **Large Surfaces (`1rem` / 16px)**: Modals, primary analytical dashboards, and executive overview panels.
- **Micro Tokens (`0.25rem` / 4px)**: Nested progress bars, table cell tags, and inline financial code tokens.
- **Capsule Exception**: Standalone status pills (e.g., "LIVE BRIEFING") remain fully rounded pill shapes to sharply contrast with orthogonal card geometries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Sovereign Blue (`#2563eb`) with white Oswald text (`label-lg`, uppercase), `padding: 0.75rem 1.5rem`, `border-radius: 0.5rem`. Sharp transitions with a 2px offset ring on focus.
- **Secondary**: Transparent with 1px border (`#0f172a`), Midnight Slate text.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Clean text with minimal inset hover (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.05)`).

### Metric & Executive Cards
- Built on `#FFFFFF` backgrounds with an 8px radius and a structural 1px border.
- Headers integrate condensed Oswald metric values accompanied by an upper-right Playwrite script micro-note (e.g., *Executive Tier* or *Cohort Alpha*).

### Chips & Badges
- Strict 8px or pill radius with Oswald typography (`label-md`, tracking +0.08em). 
- Backgrounds utilize muted tinted fills (`#eff6ff` for Blue, `#f0fdf4` for Jade) paired with solid 1px matching border tones.

### Inputs & Select Fields
- Clean `#FFFFFF` fill with an 8px radius, `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)` border, and Work Sans text for input values.
- Float labels use Oswald (`label-md`) in Midnight Slate, highlighting to `#2563eb` on focus.

### Lists & Curriculum Timelines
- Separated by hairline borders (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`).
- Time stamps, module counts, and quantitative indices set strictly in Oswald numerals; module descriptions set in Work Sans.
